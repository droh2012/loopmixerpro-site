Loop Mixer Pro – Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026

Loop Mixer Pro is made by David Hewitt ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

What we collect

Nothing. Loop Mixer Pro does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.

Your recordings

Everything you record or import stays on your iPhone, inside the app. We never see, receive or upload your recordings. If you choose to export or share a recording, it goes only where you send it.

Microphone

The app asks for microphone access so you can record your voice and instruments. Recording happens only when you start it.

Recording from another app

If you use "Record from Another App," iOS lets the app capture the sound playing in another app while the broadcast is on. That audio is saved only on your device. The microphone is not used for this.

Backups

If iCloud Backup is on, iOS may include the app's recordings in your device backup, under Apple's own privacy terms. We have no access to these backups.

Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.

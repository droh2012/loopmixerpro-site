Play a mix on a schedule

Loop Mixer Pro can start a saved mix by itself at a set time, using Apple's Shortcuts app. Set it up once; it then runs even with the phone locked.

  1. Save a mix first. On the Player, load a voice and a music track, then tap Save Mix (top right).
  2. Open Shortcuts, tap Automation at the bottom, then + (or New Automation).
  3. Tap Time of Day. Set the time, then under Repeat choose Daily, Weekly (tick the days) or Monthly.
  4. Choose Run Immediately and tap Next.
  5. Tap New Blank Automation → Add Action, search “Loop Mixer” and tap Play Mix.
  6. Tap the blue word Mix to choose your mix. Tap Minutes and enter how long it should loop, or leave it empty to use the mix's Play for time.
  7. Tap Done. At that time the mix starts, loops, fades out and stops.

To stop at a set time too, add a second automation with the action “Stop Loop Mixer Pro”. You can also say “Hey Siri, play a mix in Loop Mixer Pro”. This guide is also in the app: tap the timer button under Play, then Play on a Schedule.

Common questions

Where are my recordings stored?
Only on your iPhone, inside the app. Nothing is uploaded.

How do I record audio from another app?
On the Record tab, tap Record from Another App, pick Loop Mixer Pro, tap Start Broadcast, then play audio in the other app. Stop it from the red pill in the status bar. The recording appears in your Library.

How do I organize recordings?
In the Library, tap New Folder in the Voice or Music section, then swipe a recording and tap Folder to move it.

How do I get a recording out of the app?
In the Library, swipe right on a recording and tap Export.
